Frequently asked

How do I get the files after paying?
After the PayPal payment is complete, come back to this page and click “I’ve paid — open my files”. The download page opens with all 9 designs, the mockups and a one-click ZIP.
Are the files really print-ready?
Yes. Every artwork is a 3000 × 3000 px PNG at 300 DPI with a transparent background — exactly what print shops and print-on-demand platforms expect. Nothing to upscale, nothing to cut out.
Can I sell the shirts I print?
Yes. The license is commercial and non-exclusive: print and sell as many shirts as you like, on any colour. The only restriction is reselling, sharing or bundling the artwork files themselves.
Which printers and platforms work?
Any of them. DTG, DTF and screen print all accept these files, and so do Printful, Printify, Gelato and Shopify’s POD apps. The included mockups are there for your product listings.
Can I change colours or add text?
Absolutely. The files are yours: recolour, resize, add your own slogan or brand name before printing.
Do you offer refunds?
This is a digital product delivered instantly, so there are no refunds after download. If something goes wrong with the payment or the files, contact us and we’ll sort it out.
