⏸ THE LONG DRIVE 3D — GUIDE & OPTIONS

🚶 On-Foot Controls

  • WASD / Arrows: Walk (Hold Shift to Sprint, C to Crouch, Space to Jump)
  • Click 3D View: Lock Mouse Look (Press Esc to unlock cursor)
  • [E]: Pick Up Item / Open Door, Hood, Trunk / Sit in Driver's Seat
  • [F] or Left-Click: Pour Held Fluid into Cap / Eat or Drink / Scrub Rust / Spray Paint / Swap Engine, Radiator, or Wheel
  • [1–5] or Scroll: Select Hotbar Slot • [G]: Drop Held Item
  • [L]: Toggle Flashlight

🚗 Driving Controls

  • [W / S]: Throttle / Brake & Reverse
  • [A / D]: Steer Left / Right
  • [Space]: Handbrake (Hold to drift, releases automatically on gas)
  • [E]: Step Out of Vehicle
  • [V]: Toggle 1st-Person Cockpit / 3rd-Person Chase Camera
  • [I]: Ignition Start/Stop • [L]: Headlights (Off/Low/High)
  • [R]: Radio On/Off • [F / N]: Next Radio Station • [H]: Horn
  • [U]: Recover / Flip Car Upright onto Highway
