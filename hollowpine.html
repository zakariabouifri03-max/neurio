A FEW THINGS WORTH REMEMBERING

Read the wild.

YOUR FIRST NIGHT

Start small.
Stay breathing.

Gather fallen branches, loose stones, and wild flax with E. You can also chop marked pine trees: four hatchet swings yield five wood.

Build a little warmth.

Collect 4 wood + 4 stone, then open Crafting near camp. A campfire restores warmth, lights your torch, and repels wolves. Add wood with E to keep it burning.

Make it to morning.

A shelter needs 8 wood + 4 fiber. Build it, keep your fire lit, and press E by the shelter to rest until dawn. Eat and drink before you turn in.

Respect the dark.

Time moves faster here. Nights are cold, and wolves get curious. Stay near a fire or carry your lit torch. Hunger, thirst, and cold can cost you health.

An original, stylized survival prototype inspired by the forest-survival genre. Not affiliated with Endnight Games or The Forest. No multiplayer, cave system, or borrowed game assets.
