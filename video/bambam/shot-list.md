# Shot list — BamBam, 15.0 s, 9:16, single unbroken take

**Camera:** locked-off smartphone, slightly elevated bedside angle, framing
BamBam's face + both hands + teddy + upper body. Nearly stationary; only the
micro-drift of a hand-held phone. No cuts, no zooms, no camera moves.

---

## 0.0 – 3.0 s · Peaceful sleep

| Element | State |
|---|---|
| BamBam | On his side, asleep, cheek already near the bear's head |
| Eyes | Fully closed — never open, not even a flicker |
| Arms | Both loosely embracing the teddy |
| Breathing | Chest rises and falls, very subtle and slow |
| Teddy | Resting against the hoodie, slightly compressed by his arm weight |

The opening frame **is** your reference image. Establish it and hold it.

## 3.0 – 6.0 s · Tiny sleepy adjustment

| Element | Change |
|---|---|
| Fingers | Flex and curl into the plush fabric |
| One arm | Draws a little closer to his chest |
| Teddy | Shifts ~2–3 cm toward him; fur compresses where his hand presses |
| Head | Small shift against the pillow |
| Eyes | Still fully closed. He does not wake. |

Unconscious. No big movement — this reads as a reflexive settling, not a decision.

## 6.0 – 9.0 s · Pulling the teddy closer

| Element | Change |
|---|---|
| Both arms | Bend at the elbow, draw the bear in |
| Quality | Gentle, slightly clumsy, believably unconscious |
| Shoulders | Shift subtly against the mattress |
| Teddy | Slides across the sheet **with friction** — it drags, it doesn't glide |
| Target | Bear's head arrives near his cheek |

Key physics note: the toy is **dragged**, not lifted. It stays in contact with
the sheet the whole way. No abrupt lift, no human-like carrying gesture.

## 9.0 – 12.0 s · The nuzzle

| Element | Change |
|---|---|
| Cheek | Presses into the plush head |
| Motion | **One** small nuzzle — a brief rub, then stop |
| Fingers | Relax slightly while still holding on |
| Face | Completely relaxed, eyes closed, natural breathing |

This is the money beat. Keep it small. An exaggerated or repeated nuzzle tips
straight into "staged" and loses the whole clip.

## 12.0 – 15.0 s · Back to stillness

| Element | Change |
|---|---|
| Body | Settles into the pillow |
| Arm 1 | Wrapped around the teddy |
| Arm 2 | Hand resting softly on the plush body |
| Cheek | Still nestled against the bear's head |
| Bedding | Settles after the movement |
| Motion | Almost nil — breathing only |

Final frame: peacefully hugging the bear against his face, fast asleep.

---

## Continuity checklist

Watch the playback specifically for these — they're the failure modes:

- [ ] Eyes never open, at any frame
- [ ] Hoodie is the same charcoal-gray from frame 0 to frame 450
- [ ] Fur colour doesn't shift between beats
- [ ] Teddy design is constant — same ear size, same eye placement, same stitch
- [ ] Teddy never moves on its own; it only moves when his arms move it
- [ ] Hand count and finger count constant (count them at 0 s and at 15 s)
- [ ] No float, no stretch, no shape change on the plush
- [ ] Background is stable — no warping sheets, no morphing pillow
- [ ] Audio is room tone only: no music, no voice, no cartoon "aww" SFX
