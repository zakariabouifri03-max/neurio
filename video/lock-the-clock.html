The story

  • 1973 oil embargo → Nixon makes DST year-round (Jan 6, 1974).
  • Sunrise passed 9 a.m. in the north; kids walked to school in the dark.
  • 8 Florida schoolchildren killed vs 2 the year before; support 79%→42%.
  • Energy savings ~1% — disputed. Rolled back Oct 27, 1974 (294 days).
