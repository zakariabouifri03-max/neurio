THE ROSIE KITCHEN GUIDE

A few good things to know.

The recipes use familiar U.S. kitchen measures first. Metric amounts are practical approximations; for baking, a scale is the surest way to get consistent results.

MEASUREMENTS

  • 1 cup liquid = 240 ml
  • 1 tablespoon = 15 ml
  • 1 teaspoon = 5 ml
  • 1 ounce = about 28 g
  • Spoon flour into the cup and level it; don't pack it.

OVEN CONVERSIONS

  • 325°F = 165°C
  • 350°F = 175°C
  • 375°F = 190°C
  • 400°F = 200°C
  • 425°F = 220°C

SAFE TEMPERATURES

  • Ground beef: 160°F / 71°C
  • Poultry: 165°F / 74°C
  • Reheated leftovers: 165°F / 74°C
  • Use an instant-read thermometer in the thickest part.

A FEW KITCHEN HABITS

  • Preheat the oven fully.
  • Grate cheese from a block for smoother melting.
  • Keep raw meat separate from ready-to-eat foods.
  • Refrigerate perishable leftovers within 2 hours.

People with food allergies should check every package label and adapt the recipe safely.

RECIPE 01 · BURGERS & SANDWICHES

Grandma Rosie's Smash Burger

A crisp-edged, juicy burger with the kind of cheese pull that ends the conversation.

PREP10 min COOK10 min TOTAL20 min MAKES4 burgers

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 lb (450 g) 80/20 ground beef, cold
  • 4 soft burger buns, split
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 1 tsp kosher salt, divided
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp neutral oil, for the pan
  • 1/4 cup (60 g) mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp ketchup
  • 1 tsp yellow mustard
  • 1 tsp pickle brine, plus dill pickles to serve

YOU'LL NEED 12-inch cast-iron or heavy skillet, sturdy spatula, parchment paper

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD05
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 01 · BURGERS & SANDWICHES

Let's make it.

Grandma Rosie's Smash Burger 20 min total

  1. 01

    Mix the Rosie sauce

    Stir the mayonnaise, ketchup, mustard, and pickle brine together. Set it near the stove so the burgers can be assembled while hot.

  2. 02

    Toast the buns

    Heat the dry skillet over medium heat. Toast the cut sides of the buns until golden, about 45 seconds. Set aside.

  3. 03

    Smash for the crust

    Raise the heat to high and add the oil. Divide beef into four loose balls. Working one at a time, place a ball in the pan, cover with parchment, and press firmly with a sturdy spatula until about 1/4 inch (6 mm) thick. Season with salt and pepper.

  4. 04

    Flip and melt

    Cook without moving for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes, until the edges are deeply browned. Scrape underneath, flip, add a slice of cheese, and cook 45 to 60 seconds more, until the beef reaches 160°F (71°C) and the cheese melts.

  5. 05

    Build the burger

    Spread sauce on the buns, add the hot patty and pickles, close the bun, and serve right away. For a double, stack two patties per bun.

ROSIE'S TIP

Don't pack the beef into a tight meatball. Loose beef makes the lacy, crispy edges that a thick patty can only dream about.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Best eaten immediately. Refrigerate cooked patties in a sealed container for up to 3 days; reheat in a skillet until steaming hot.

SERVE IT WITH

Serve with crinkle-cut fries, a dill pickle, and extra Rosie sauce.

RECIPE 02 · COMFORT FOOD

Three-Ingredient Stovetop Mac & Cheese

Creamy, cozy, and made without a powdered orange packet in sight.

PREP5 min COOK15 min TOTAL20 min MAKES4

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 8 oz (225 g) elbow macaroni
  • 3 cups (720 ml) whole milk, plus a splash if needed
  • 2 cups (200 g) sharp cheddar, freshly grated
  • Kosher salt, optional and not counted among the three main ingredients

YOU'LL NEED Medium heavy-bottomed saucepan, wooden spoon, box grater

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD07
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 02 · COMFORT FOOD

Let's make it.

Three-Ingredient Stovetop Mac & Cheese 20 min total

  1. 01

    Warm the pasta and milk

    Put the macaroni and milk in a heavy saucepan. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat, stirring often and scraping the corners so nothing sticks.

  2. 02

    Cook until just tender

    Keep the milk at a quiet simmer, never a hard boil. Cook 9 to 12 minutes, stirring every 30 seconds, until the pasta is al dente and most of the milk has thickened around it.

  3. 03

    Make it cheesy

    Turn off the heat. Add the cheddar in three handfuls, stirring until each addition melts before adding the next. Taste and add a small pinch of salt if the cheese needs it.

  4. 04

    Rest and serve

    Let the pot stand for 2 minutes; the sauce will cling to the noodles as it settles. If it becomes too thick, stir in warm milk 1 tablespoon at a time.

ROSIE'S TIP

Grate the cheese yourself. Pre-shredded cheese is coated to keep it separate, and that coating can make a silky sauce turn grainy.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat gently on the stove with 1 to 2 tablespoons of milk per serving; do not boil.

SERVE IT WITH

Add a green salad or roasted broccoli if you want to pretend this is a balanced dinner. I won't tell.

RECIPE 03 · FAST-FOOD FAVORITES

Air-Fryer Crispy Chicken Tenders

Crunchy, tender, and ready for a dunk in honey mustard.

PREP20 min COOK12 min TOTAL32 min MAKES4

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) chicken tenderloins
  • 1 cup (240 ml) buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3/4 cup (95 g) all-purpose flour
  • 1 1/2 cups (90 g) panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Neutral-oil spray
  • For dipping: 1/4 cup mayonnaise, 1 tbsp honey, and 1 tsp Dijon mustard

YOU'LL NEED Air fryer, three shallow bowls, instant-read thermometer

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD09
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 03 · FAST-FOOD FAVORITES

Let's make it.

Air-Fryer Crispy Chicken Tenders 32 min total

  1. 01

    Season the chicken

    Pat the tenders dry. Whisk the buttermilk and egg in one bowl. In a second bowl, combine flour, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Put panko in a third bowl.

  2. 02

    Bread each tender

    Coat a piece of chicken in seasoned flour, dip it in the buttermilk mixture, then press it into the panko. Set on a rack and repeat. Let the coating sit for 5 minutes so it adheres.

  3. 03

    Air-fry in batches

    Heat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange tenders in one layer with space between them; spray the tops lightly with oil. Cook 10 to 12 minutes, turning once, until deeply golden and 165°F (74°C) inside.

  4. 04

    Stir the dip

    While the chicken cooks, stir mayonnaise, honey, and Dijon together. Add a pinch of salt if desired. Serve the tenders hot with the dip.

ROSIE'S TIP

If the basket is crowded, the coating steams instead of crisping. Give each tender a little personal space; even chicken needs boundaries.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Re-crisp in an air fryer at 350°F (175°C) for 3 to 5 minutes; avoid microwaving if you want to keep the crunch.

SERVE IT WITH

Try honey mustard, ranch, barbecue sauce, or a squeeze of lemon with crunchy slaw.

Times are estimates. Oven and air-fryer performance varies by model.

RECIPE 04 · BREAKFAST & BRUNCH

Fluffy American Buttermilk Pancakes

Tall, tender pancakes with crisp buttery edges and real maple syrup.

PREP10 min COOK15 min TOTAL25 min MAKES4 (about 8 pancakes)

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 1/2 cups (188 g) all-purpose flour
  • 2 tbsp (25 g) granulated sugar
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp baking soda
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1 1/4 cups (300 ml) buttermilk
  • 1 large egg
  • 3 tbsp (42 g) unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • Butter and maple syrup, for serving

YOU'LL NEED Two mixing bowls, whisk, nonstick skillet or griddle

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD11
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 04 · BREAKFAST & BRUNCH

Let's make it.

Fluffy American Buttermilk Pancakes 25 min total

  1. 01

    Whisk the dry ingredients

    In a large bowl, whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt until evenly combined.

  2. 02

    Mix the wet ingredients

    In another bowl, whisk the buttermilk, egg, melted butter, and vanilla. Pour into the dry ingredients and fold just until no dry flour remains. A few small lumps are welcome.

  3. 03

    Heat the griddle

    Warm a lightly buttered skillet over medium heat, about 350°F (175°C). A drop of water should dance and disappear, not smoke.

  4. 04

    Cook the pancakes

    Pour 1/4 cup (60 ml) batter for each pancake. Cook until bubbles appear and the edges look set, about 2 minutes. Flip once and cook 1 to 2 minutes more until golden and springy.

  5. 05

    Serve warm

    Stack the pancakes, add butter, and drizzle with maple syrup. Keep finished pancakes on a rack in a 200°F (95°C) oven while you cook the rest.

ROSIE'S TIP

Stop mixing when the flour disappears. Smooth batter makes flat pancakes; a few lumps make the tall, fluffy ones.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Refrigerate cooked pancakes for up to 3 days or freeze between parchment sheets for 2 months. Reheat in a toaster or a 300°F (150°C) oven.

SERVE IT WITH

Fresh berries, sliced bananas, crisp bacon, or a spoonful of peanut butter all feel right at the breakfast table.

Times are estimates. Oven and air-fryer performance varies by model.

RECIPE 05 · BREAKFAST & BRUNCH

Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Sandwich

The diner-counter classic you can make before the coffee gets cold.

PREP8 min COOK12 min TOTAL20 min MAKES4 sandwiches

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 4 English muffins, split
  • 8 slices bacon
  • 4 large eggs
  • 4 slices American cheese
  • 1 tbsp (14 g) unsalted butter
  • Kosher salt and black pepper
  • Hot sauce or ketchup, optional

YOU'LL NEED Large skillet, small nonstick skillet, paper towels

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD13
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 05 · BREAKFAST & BRUNCH

Let's make it.

Bacon, Egg & Cheese Breakfast Sandwich 20 min total

  1. 01

    Cook the bacon

    Place bacon in a cold skillet and set over medium heat. Cook, turning as needed, until crisp, 8 to 10 minutes. Transfer to paper towels and pour off all but 1 teaspoon of the drippings.

  2. 02

    Toast the muffins

    Toast the English muffins cut-side down in the same skillet until golden, 1 to 2 minutes. Keep warm.

  3. 03

    Fry the eggs

    Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat. Crack in the eggs, season lightly, and cook until the whites are fully set and the yolks are cooked to your preference. For a neat stack, break the yolks and fold each egg into a square; cook until no liquid egg remains.

  4. 04

    Melt and stack

    Place a slice of cheese on each hot egg for 30 seconds. Set the eggs on the toasted muffin bottoms, add two bacon slices, close the sandwiches, and serve.

ROSIE'S TIP

Low heat gives you tender eggs. High heat gives you a rubber coaster with ambitions.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

For meal prep, cool the cooked eggs and bacon completely, then refrigerate separately for up to 3 days. Assemble and reheat just before eating.

SERVE IT WITH

Add a sliced tomato, a little hot sauce, or a hash-brown patty tucked right inside.

Times are estimates. Oven and air-fryer performance varies by model.

RECIPE 06 · FAST-FOOD FAVORITES

Cheesy Weeknight Beef Tacos

Seasoned beef, crunchy shells, and enough cheddar to make Tuesday feel festive.

PREP10 min COOK15 min TOTAL25 min MAKES4 (8 tacos)

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 lb (450 g) lean ground beef
  • 8 crunchy taco shells
  • 1 tbsp neutral oil, if needed
  • 2 tsp chili powder
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 3/4 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) water
  • 1 1/2 cups (150 g) shredded cheddar
  • 1 cup shredded lettuce, 1 diced tomato, and sour cream, for topping

YOU'LL NEED Large skillet, sheet pan, small bowls for toppings

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD15
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 06 · FAST-FOOD FAVORITES

Let's make it.

Cheesy Weeknight Beef Tacos 25 min total

  1. 01

    Brown the beef

    Heat a skillet over medium-high. Add the beef and cook, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains and the meat reaches 160°F (71°C), about 6 to 8 minutes. Drain excess fat if needed.

  2. 02

    Season and simmer

    Stir in chili powder, cumin, paprika, garlic powder, salt, and water. Simmer uncovered for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring occasionally, until glossy and lightly saucy.

  3. 03

    Warm the shells

    Arrange taco shells on a sheet pan and warm according to the package directions, usually 3 to 5 minutes at 350°F (175°C).

  4. 04

    Fill and finish

    Divide the beef among the shells. Top with cheddar, lettuce, tomato, and sour cream. Serve at once while the shells are crisp.

ROSIE'S TIP

Let the beef simmer with the spices for a few minutes. A packet dumped over dry meat is not a seasoning strategy; it's a cry for help.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Refrigerate the beef separately for up to 3 days. Reheat until steaming hot, then fill freshly warmed shells so they stay crunchy.

SERVE IT WITH

Set out salsa, lime wedges, pickled jalapeños, and guacamole so everyone can build their own.

RECIPE 07 · SNACKS & SIDES

Garlic-Butter Pizza Bread

Crispy baguette, garlicky butter, marinara, and bubbling mozzarella.

PREP10 min COOK15 min TOTAL25 min MAKES4 to 6

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 large French baguette (about 12 oz / 340 g)
  • 4 tbsp (56 g) unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely grated
  • 1/2 tsp Italian seasoning
  • 1 cup (240 ml) marinara sauce
  • 2 cups (200 g) shredded low-moisture mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan
  • Fresh basil, for finishing

YOU'LL NEED Rimmed sheet pan, small bowl, sharp bread knife

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD17
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 07 · SNACKS & SIDES

Let's make it.

Garlic-Butter Pizza Bread 25 min total

  1. 01

    Heat the oven

    Set a rack in the center and heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Line a sheet pan with parchment.

  2. 02

    Make garlic butter

    Stir the softened butter, grated garlic, and Italian seasoning together. Split the baguette lengthwise and place cut-side up on the pan.

  3. 03

    Toast the bread

    Spread the garlic butter over the cut surfaces. Bake 4 to 5 minutes, just until the edges begin to crisp. This quick toast helps keep the bread from turning soggy.

  4. 04

    Top and bake

    Spoon marinara over the bread, leaving a narrow border. Scatter mozzarella and Parmesan evenly. Bake 8 to 10 minutes, until the cheese bubbles and the edges are golden.

  5. 05

    Slice and serve

    Rest 2 minutes, add basil, and cut into thick pieces with a serrated knife.

ROSIE'S TIP

Toast the bread before adding sauce. Soggy pizza bread is just a very confused sponge.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Store leftovers in the refrigerator for up to 2 days. Reheat on a sheet pan at 375°F (190°C) for 6 to 8 minutes; the microwave softens the crust.

SERVE IT WITH

A bowl of marinara for dipping and a simple green salad make this a proper lunch.

Loaded Cheesy Fries

Crispy fries, melted cheddar, smoky bacon, cool sour cream, and green onion.

PREP10 min COOK30 min TOTAL40 min MAKES4 as a snack

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 1 1/2 lb (680 g) frozen straight-cut fries
  • 1 cup (100 g) sharp cheddar, freshly grated
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked crisp and crumbled
  • 1/3 cup (80 g) sour cream
  • 2 green onions, thinly sliced
  • 1 small jalapeño, seeded and sliced, optional
  • Black pepper, to taste

YOU'LL NEED Sheet pan or air fryer, oven-safe serving dish, skillet

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD19
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 08 · SNACKS & SIDES

Let's make it.

Loaded Cheesy Fries 40 min total

  1. 01

    Crisp the fries

    Heat the oven to the temperature on the fry package, usually 425°F (220°C). Spread fries in a single layer on a preheated sheet pan and bake, turning once, until deeply golden and crisp, about 25 to 30 minutes. Air-fry in batches if preferred.

  2. 02

    Cook the bacon

    While the fries bake, cook bacon in a skillet over medium heat until crisp. Drain on paper towels, then crumble.

  3. 03

    Melt the cheese

    Pile the hot fries in an oven-safe dish. Scatter cheddar and bacon over the top. Return to the oven for 2 to 3 minutes, just until the cheese melts.

  4. 04

    Finish and share

    Dollop with sour cream and finish with green onions, jalapeño, and black pepper. Serve immediately with forks for the table.

ROSIE'S TIP

Crisp the fries first and add cheese second. If you cover them from the start, you get steamed potato sticks. Nobody ordered that.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

These are best straight from the oven. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 2 days and re-crisp uncovered at 400°F (200°C) for 8 to 10 minutes.

SERVE IT WITH

Add ranch or barbecue sauce on the side. For a heartier plate, top with leftover taco beef or pulled chicken.

RECIPE 09 · COMFORT FOOD

Crispy Two-Cheese Grilled Cheese

Buttery, golden bread around a blanket of stretchy cheddar and Monterey Jack.

PREP8 min COOK12 min TOTAL20 min MAKES4 sandwiches

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 8 slices sourdough or sturdy white bread
  • 1 1/2 cups (150 g) sharp cheddar, grated
  • 1 cup (100 g) Monterey Jack, grated
  • 4 tbsp (56 g) softened butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard, optional
  • Tomato soup, for serving

YOU'LL NEED Large skillet or griddle, spatula, box grater

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD21
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 09 · COMFORT FOOD

Let's make it.

Crispy Two-Cheese Grilled Cheese 20 min total

  1. 01

    Build the sandwiches

    Mix cheddar and Monterey Jack. Spread butter on one side of each bread slice. Turn four slices butter-side down; spread a thin swipe of Dijon on the inside if using and add the cheese. Close with the remaining bread, butter-side out.

  2. 02

    Cook low and slow

    Heat a skillet over medium-low. Cook sandwiches in batches for 3 to 4 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until the bread is evenly golden.

  3. 03

    Let the cheese catch up

    If the bread is browning before the cheese melts, lower the heat and cover the skillet for 1 minute. Remove the lid for the last 30 seconds to keep the crust crisp.

  4. 04

    Slice and serve

    Rest each sandwich for 1 minute, then cut diagonally. Serve with hot tomato soup for dunking.

ROSIE'S TIP

Low heat is the secret. A sandwich is not late for work; give the cheese time to melt before you rush it.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

Grilled cheese is best fresh. If needed, cool and refrigerate for up to 1 day, then reheat in a dry skillet over low heat until crisp and hot.

SERVE IT WITH

Classic tomato soup, dill pickles, or a handful of kettle chips.

Buffalo Chicken Crunch Wraps

Spicy chicken, cool ranch, crunchy lettuce, and plenty of cheddar in a toasted wrap.

PREP12 min COOK8 min TOTAL20 min MAKES4 wraps

Ingredients

Gather these first
  • 2 cups (about 300 g) cooked shredded chicken
  • 1/3 cup (80 ml) Buffalo wing sauce
  • 4 large flour tortillas (10 inches / 25 cm)
  • 1 cup (100 g) shredded cheddar
  • 1 cup finely shredded romaine lettuce
  • 1/2 cup (120 ml) ranch or blue-cheese dressing
  • 1 tbsp unsalted butter
  • Celery sticks, to serve

YOU'LL NEED Small saucepan, large skillet or griddle, tongs

AMERICAN COMFORT FOOD23
GRANDMA ROSIEFROM ROSIE'S KITCHEN

RECIPE 10 · FAST-FOOD FAVORITES

Let's make it.

Buffalo Chicken Crunch Wraps 20 min total

  1. 01

    Warm the Buffalo chicken

    Combine the cooked chicken and wing sauce in a small saucepan over medium-low heat. Stir until hot, about 3 minutes. Taste before adding extra salt; bottled sauce is already seasoned.

  2. 02

    Fill the tortillas

    Warm tortillas briefly in a dry skillet so they bend without cracking. Place chicken down the center of each, then add cheddar, lettuce, and a drizzle of dressing.

ROSIE'S TIP

Put the seam down first. Gravity is a free kitchen helper, and it keeps the good bits from escaping.

MAKE AHEAD & STORAGE

SERVE IT WITH

Celery sticks, carrot ribbons, and extra ranch for dipping.

Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies About 70 min total

  1. 01

    Whisk the dry mix

    In a bowl, whisk flour, baking soda, and salt. Set aside. Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line two baking sheets with parchment.

  2. 02

    Cream butter and sugars

    Beat butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium speed for 2 minutes, until light and fluffy. Scrape the bowl. Beat in eggs one at a time, then vanilla.

Cottage Cheese Crust Pizza 40 min total
