## 🎯 Why these 10 niches (low competition)

Each design targets a **micro-niche**, not a broad category — the 2026 Etsy rule is
"the more specific the audience, the lower the competition". Research summary:

- **Pet breed / exotic-pet niches** — a generic "dog mom" tee competes with 50,000+
  listings; "Bernese Dog Mom" or "Hedgehog Mom" competes with a few hundred. Buyer
  intent is identical, competition drops ~99%.
- **Fast-growing hobby niches** — disc golf, pickleball (fastest-growing US sport),
  mahjong (millennial/Gen-Z "mahjong night" trend), birdwatching (+30% since 2020),
  homebrewing, amateur radio — big retailers don't serve these communities.
- **Profession/identity micro-niches** — sourdough bakers, stargazers, ham operators:
  passionate repeat buyers that actively search for "their" design.

All designs use **original wording** (no famous slogans, no brand names, no IP risk).

## 🚀 How to use

1. **POD route (recommended):** upload the transparent PNG to Printful/Printify →
   create tee, hoodie, mug, tote → connect to Etsy. No stock, no shipping.
2. **Digital download:** zip the PNGs + a small PDF and sell as "printable art".
3. **Listing photos:** use `previews/*.jpg` (design on cream, 3000×3000) — Etsy
   recommends square listing images at least 2000×2000 px.
4. **Pricing:** POD apparel $24–34, mugs $18–26, digital downloads $5–12.
   Micro-niches support premium pricing — don't race to the bottom.
