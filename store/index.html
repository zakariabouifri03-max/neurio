Still unsure? Write to hello@atlasink.studio — a human answers.

What exactly do I get?

A ZIP with four files per design: an editable SVG, a high-resolution transparent PNG at 300 DPI, a DTF-ready PNG with a white underbase, and an EPS for older vector software. A license PDF is included too.

Can I sell shirts with these designs?

Yes. Every purchase includes a commercial license for up to 500 printed pieces per design. Need unlimited units? The extended license is +$39 per design.

Do you print or ship physical shirts?

No — this is a digital-only studio. You get the print files, not the garment. That's why delivery is instant and prices stay low.

What software do I need?

Any vector editor will do: Illustrator, CorelDRAW, Affinity Designer or Inkscape. If your printer only accepts images, the 300 DPI PNG and DTF files are ready to go.

Can I get a refund?

Digital files can't be returned, so all sales are final. If a file is broken or won't open, email us and we'll fix it or replace it the same day. Try the free design first to be sure.
