## Download

Download **Neurio-0.1.0-preview.apk** from the assets below and open it on your Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from the browser you used. Android 8.0+ and an updated Android System WebView are required.

## Included

- Neurio's offline creator workspace, in English and Moroccan Darija.
- Content drafts, local video preview and manual five-point content review.
- Posting planner, goals with saved progress, local coach, and six template tools.
- Android file picker, clipboard and JSON workspace export.
- Bundled assets: this app does not require a hosted website.

## Important

This is a **development-signed preview**, not a Play Store production build. Dashboard statistics and sample scores are demonstrations. Real Instagram authorization, automated video analysis, and an AI model are **not connected**. No follower count is guaranteed.

No Instagram password is requested. Workspace data stays in this app on this phone. Uninstalling or clearing app data deletes it; export before uninstalling. Subsequent development builds may require uninstall/reinstall because preview signing keys are temporary. Export files cannot yet be re-imported through the app.

CI compiles the app and verifies its APK signature and package metadata. This release has not been tested on a physical Android device. The included SHA-256 file can be used to verify the download.
