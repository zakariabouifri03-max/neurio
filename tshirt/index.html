Fabric & care

100% GOTS-certified organic cotton, ring-spun and enzyme-washed. Pre-shrunk ±1%. Machine wash cold, inside-out; tumble low or hang dry. The ribbed collar is knitted, not cut — it keeps its shape wash after wash.

Shipping & returns

Cut-to-order in 48h, delivered in 3–6 days. Free shipping worldwide, free 60-day returns — even on printed pieces.

Impact

Organic cotton saves ~2,400 L of water per tee vs. conventional. Offcuts are re-spun, packaging is compostable, and every order funds 1 m² of restored wetland.
