# Taskly — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 9, 2026**

Taskly is built to be private by default. Your tasks, notes, reminders and
preferences are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to
Taskly servers, because Taskly does not operate any servers.

## What data Taskly stores

- **Tasks** — titles, descriptions, due dates/times, priorities, categories,
  completion state and reminder settings — are saved in an app-private SQLite
  database on your device.
- **Preferences** — your display name (optional), theme, week start and date
  format — are saved in app-private preferences on your device.
- Uninstalling the app deletes all of this data.

## Permissions Taskly asks for

- **Notifications** — requested only when you set your first task reminder,
  so Taskly can show the reminder at the right time. You can use the whole
  app without granting this.
- **Exact alarms** — an optional Android permission you may grant so
  reminders are not delayed by a few minutes. Taskly works without it and
  explains when it is unavailable.

## What Taskly never does

- No account or sign-up required.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs.
- No advertising SDKs.
- No task content ever leaves your device.
- No collection of personal, financial or sensitive data.
- No data sharing with third parties.

## Third-party software

Taskly uses open-source libraries (listed under Settings → Open-source
licenses) to provide local storage and local notifications. These libraries
run on your device and do not send your data anywhere.

## Children

Taskly does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children under 13, because no data ever leaves the device.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear in the app and in
the Google Play Data safety section before the related update ships.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach the developer through the email listed on the
Google Play store listing.
