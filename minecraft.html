How it is built

  • 64 block textures painted pixel-by-pixel on a 256×256 canvas atlas, with derived height → normal and roughness maps for real PBR shading.
  • Voxel chunks are meshed with face culling and baked per-vertex ambient occlusion (the classic 3-neighbour corner formula, with quad flipping to avoid lighting seams).
  • Grass and leaves are tinted per biome through vertex colours; four material layers keep draw calls low (opaque / cutout / water / emissive).
  • The sky dome, sun, moon, stars, ocean swell, campfire embers, fireflies and the whole soundtrack are generated from code — no assets are downloaded.
  • Lighting is image-based: a PMREM probe is re-baked from the sky a couple of times a second, so every material picks up the correct ambient colour as the day moves.
